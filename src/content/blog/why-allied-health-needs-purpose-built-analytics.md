Most allied health clinics in BC run on two systems: a scheduling and practice-management tool, and a billing
workflow layered on top of it. Both are essential, and neither was designed to answer a simple question clinic
owners ask constantly: **how is the practice actually performing?**

That gap shows up in familiar ways:

- A clinic owner exporting spreadsheets from three different systems to build a monthly summary by hand.
- A multi-discipline practice with no easy way to compare utilization across physiotherapy, massage, and
  counselling rooms.
- A billing team that can see individual claims, but not payer mix trends across MSP, extended health, ICBC, and
  WorkSafeBC over time.

None of this is a failure of the practice-management or billing tools themselves — they're built for running the
day-to-day, not for performance reporting. But as clinics grow past a single practitioner, or expand into new
disciplines and locations, that missing layer becomes a real cost: slower decisions, and less visibility into where
a clinic is actually strong or struggling.

That's the gap Datafore is building for. Not a generic BI dashboard bolted onto clinic data, but analytics designed
from the ground up around how allied health practices in BC actually operate — starting with the metrics that
matter to clinic owners and clinical leads, not just what happens to be easy to export.

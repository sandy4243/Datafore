Statistics Canada files allied health clinics under one industry code: offices of other health practitioners.
Physiotherapy, chiropractic, optometry, counselling, dietetics and many other practices sit there. We read two of its
data sets as a BC clinic owner would: how fast the sector is hiring, and how big its businesses are.

## Therapy clinics are the largest named group with staff

Among businesses with employees, offices of physical, occupational and speech therapists and audiologists are the
largest group with its own industry code. The bigger "all other" group covers practitioners without one, such as
dietitians, podiatrists and naturopaths.

## What this means for clinic owners

These are province-wide figures, not a picture of any one clinic. Our reading of them:

- The sector has outgrown BC's economy for a decade, and its hiring with it. Competition for practitioners is real.
- Most practices are one person or a small team, so the analysis usually falls to the owner. That is the gap
  Datafore is built for.
- A clinic with even five or ten staff is in the top tenth of the sector by size. At that scale, each empty hour and
  each patient who stops coming shows up in the month's results.

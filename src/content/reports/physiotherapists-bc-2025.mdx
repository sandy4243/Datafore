## What this means for clinic owners

These are province-wide figures, not a picture of any one clinic. Three things stand out:

- Most physiotherapy in BC now happens in community settings. Competition for patients, and for staff, is mostly
  between clinics.
- Where physiotherapists are scarce, as in Fraser East, the Northern Interior and the Northeast, a clinic faces less
  competition for patients and a smaller pool to hire from.
- One in three BC physiotherapists was educated outside Canada, so credential recognition and onboarding matter
  when you hire.
